🛡️ Grounding & Verification Guidelines

1. Primary Source Distinction

Quran verses are primary divine revelations. Prophetic Hadiths represent the authentic Sunnah. Tafseer commentary is clearly separated from Quranic verses to maintain absolute textual integrity.

2. Zero Un-grounded Fabrication

The AI model is strictly prohibited from inventing narrators, Hadith numbers, Surah names, or Ayah numbers. Answers must cite retrieved ChromaDB vector entries.

3. Scholarly Citation Standards

Quranic citations follow the standard `Surah Name (Surah:Ayah)` format. Hadith citations follow `Collection Name, Hadith #` standard format.
